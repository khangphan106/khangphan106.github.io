Aquabloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Aquabloom ("the app") is a calm aquarium game for all ages. This page explains what information is used when you play it. In short: we don't ask for, collect or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal details.

What stays on your device

  • Your aquarium, coins, settings and progress are saved only on your device.
  • Photos you take in the app are saved in the app's own folder on your device (visible in the Files app). They are never uploaded.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

The app can show a short video ad, only when the player taps a button to get a bonus (for example, double coins). There are no banner or pop-up ads. These ads come from Google AdMob, and every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (GDPR), with the content rating limited to general audiences (G). This means:

  • No personalised or interest-based ads, and no ad tracking across apps.
  • The app never asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency).
  • To deliver an ad and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process limited technical information, such as the device type, operating system version, app version, an approximate (city-level) location derived from the IP address, and basic diagnostics. This is not linked to you and is not used to build a profile. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

The optional Supporter Pack is sold by Apple through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app only learns whether the pack was bought. A grown-up check appears before any purchase.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents can contact us with any question using the address below.

Changes
